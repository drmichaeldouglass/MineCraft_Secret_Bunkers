## Audio

Eleven original mono WAV assets are mapped through `resource_pack/sounds/sound_definitions.json`.

`access_granted`, `access_denied`, `alarm_alert`, `alarm_lockdown`, `blast_door_movement`, `blast_door_warning`, `elevator_move`, `elevator_arrive`, `laser_turret_fire`, `warning_beep`, `destruction`.

`warning_beep` is available for future countdown polish; the other sound events are wired to the shared systems. No third-party audio is included.

## Structures

43 authored/generated binary templates: 19 furnished room roles (bedrooms, bunk rooms, lounges, mess halls, hydroponic farms, mining faces, workshops, labs and more) in north/south connector orientations, four hall/core modules and the surface entrance. Rooms use vanilla furniture with block entity data for bed colours. See `module_catalog.json`. Programmed overlays add doors, readers, controls, grids, shafts, escape route, turrets, the covering mountain and TNT demolition charges.

## Other

Both packs include an original 128×128 bunker icon and en_US names. Advanced control models, damaged turret models, directional animated doors, facility-specific palettes and cinematic particles are deferred.
